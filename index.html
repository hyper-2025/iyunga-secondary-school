Building Skills, Knowledge & Character

Iyunga Technical Secondary School is a government secondary school located in Mbeya City, Tanzania.

The school provides secondary education together with technical and vocational-oriented learning, preparing students for further education, employment and practical life.

The school is officially identified by NECTA as examination centre P0112.
